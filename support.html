Frequently Asked Questions

My alarm didn't go off.
Make sure Hypeman has permission to schedule alarms: open iOS Settings > Hypeman and confirm Alarms/Notifications are allowed. Also confirm the alarm toggle is on inside the app.

How do I change my name or the voice used in the alarm?
Open the app and go to the Profile/Settings tab to update your name. A new audio clip will be generated the next time your alarm is scheduled.

How do I stop seeing ads?
Hypeman is currently free and ad-supported. If a Pro/ad-free option is available in the app, you can find it under Settings.

How do I delete my data?
All app data (alarm times, saved quotes, preferences) is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
