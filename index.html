Hypeman Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Hypeman ("the App") is a motivational quote and wake-up alarm app for iOS. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Hypeman does not require an account, login, or personal profile to use. The App does not collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying information.

  • Locally stored data: Your alarm times, saved quotes, and app preferences are stored on your device and are not transmitted to us.
  • Advertising data: The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier / IDFA, subject to your iOS App Tracking Transparency permission), general device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads. This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.

How Information Is Used

Data collected by AdMob is used solely to display and measure advertising within the App. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for any purpose beyond operating the App and displaying ads.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google's use of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can control ad personalization through your iOS device's App Tracking Transparency and Advertising settings.

Data Sharing

Other than the advertising data shared with Google AdMob as described above, Hypeman does not share your information with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app data (alarms, preferences) remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Deleting the App removes this locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Hypeman is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact information listed on the Hypeman App Store page.
